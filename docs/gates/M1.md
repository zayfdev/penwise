# Gate record: M1 ink engine

Branch `m1-ink-engine`, merged to `master` 2026-10-24. Verdict: **PASS**.

## Scope delivered

`penwise-core`: raw digitizer mapping, quantization, One Euro smoothing, windowed speed estimate, pressure/velocity/tilt
dynamics, outline brushes (perfect-freehand port, fountain, calligraphy and highlighter as exact flat-nib sweeps), stamp
brushes (distance/time spacing, keyed SplitMix64 jitter, procedural paper grain, tilt side-shading, spray), 13 presets,
versioned stroke codec and `.pwrec` fixture format, server raster (tiny-skia, feature `raster`) with a shared brush-sheet
renderer. `penwise-fixtures` converts raw `getevent` captures into `.pwrec`.

## Gate B: review

- First review (`agent://M1Review`): FAIL, 3 majors (spray loop non-termination on hostile params, PNG golden
  auto-bless on missing file, PNG goldens covering 3/13 presets) + minors. All fixed.
- Re-review (`agent://M1ReReview`): PASS. P3 follow-ups applied: flat-nib decimation (calligraphy 64 768 → 17 112
  vertices on the sentence fixture with exact centreline coverage), `helpers` module renamed `builders`, saturating
  sheet padding.

## Gate C: evidence (real pen data)

Recordings: `fixtures/raw/20261024-094722` (13 sections, ~482 Hz, Noris digital classic).

- `docs/gates/m1/line-01-sentence.png`: handwriting in all 13 brushes, upright.
- `docs/gates/m1/sheet-04-pressure-ramp.png`: pressure ramps; fountain width and stamp opacity follow pressure.
- `docs/gates/m1/line-07-tilt-shade.png`: tilted vs upright shading; pencil and shading brushes widen and soften when tilted.

Findings fixed during the gate: torn flat-nib outlines (bow-tie ribbon → convex-hull sweep), invisible or faint stamp
presets (airbrush, shading, spray, pencil, soft), blotchy crayon grain, weak fountain pressure response, rate-dependent
speed estimate (One Euro derivative → 10 ms windowed estimate), tolerance comparison widening `f32` to `f64`.
